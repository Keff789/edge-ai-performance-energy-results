# MLSys

Explore [split inference](../../results/split-inference/README.md), the [figure gallery](../../results/split-inference/figures/README.md) and [tables](../../results/split-inference/tables/README.md). The collection covers all seven models, three accelerator setups, Full alternatives, Native/Generic comparisons, quality, repeats and sensitivities.

These are descriptive deployment comparisons with recorded quality and timed-work differences. The entry point selects relevant result groups; it does not assert a submission, acceptance or paper-figure numbering.

The earlier [Top-2 Native splits by relative same-host gain](../../results/split-inference/tables/table3_top2_native.md) remains a separate supplementary historical selection method, with its original numerical results.

The two compact overview figures use those same selected UIDs: [baseline effect](../../results/split-inference/figures/fastest_split_baseline_comparison.pdf) ([SVG](../../results/split-inference/figures/fastest_split_baseline_comparison.svg), [PNG](../../results/split-inference/figures/fastest_split_baseline_comparison.png)) and [fastest-deployment throughput–energy comparison](../../results/split-inference/figures/fastest_deployment_tradeoff.pdf) ([SVG](../../results/split-inference/figures/fastest_deployment_tradeoff.svg), [PNG](../../results/split-inference/figures/fastest_deployment_tradeoff.png)). Global fastest covers open setup choice; same-host covers Full options on the selected split's physical host. The throughput–energy overview uses one common global Full denominator and is not the full Pareto front or an energy-minimum selection. All choices are retrospective over the measured candidates; quality, endpoint and parallelism differences remain.
