# MLSys

Explore [split inference](../../results/split-inference/README.md), the [figure gallery](../../results/split-inference/figures/README.md) and [tables](../../results/split-inference/tables/README.md). The collection covers all seven models, three accelerator setups, Full alternatives, Native/Generic comparisons, quality, repeats and sensitivities.

These are descriptive deployment comparisons with recorded quality and timed-work differences. The entry point selects relevant result groups; it does not assert a submission, acceptance or paper-figure numbering.
