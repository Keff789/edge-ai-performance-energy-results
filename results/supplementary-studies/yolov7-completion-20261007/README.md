# Historical YOLOv7 completion and stage profiles

This completed 7 October 2026 report describes a bounded software cause of slow H10/DeepX YOLOv7 completion. The H10-b066 comparison uses the same device and a 100-warmup/100-completion instrumented window. Dense decoding and repeated payload/source checks were replaced in the measured tail by the existing bound fast-completion path. Required decoding, finite-value checks and NMS remain inside task completion; the saved-output oracle runs after timing.

| H10 b066 diagnostic quantity | Original | Candidate |
|---|---:|---:|
| CPU completion, ms/image | 63.599 | 6.614 |
| Total instrumented window, ms/image | 73.531 | 19.073 |
| FIFO put blocking, ms/image | 67.488 | 0.027 |
| Queue age, ms | 281.435 | 0.192 |

Original dense decoding consumed 48.802 ms/image and payload hashing 7.829 ms/image. After the change, the observed producer cycle of 18.919 ms limited the pipeline; serial consumer work was 15.973 ms. Inclusive and exclusive times must not be added together. Submission latency with inflight 8 is not isolated NPU execution time, and these short profiles are not final FPS measurements.

The separate historical final cohort contains nine split configurations with 27 independent process repeats and six reused Full alternatives with 18 repeats; each uses 100 warmups and 1,000 completed tasks. Three Full controls remain outside those triples. The [15-row CSV](reported_results.csv) retains all repeats, medians, comparison ratios, quality labels and energy `not_measured`/NA; the [readable table](reported_results.md) rounds FPS to integers. This dated cohort does not replace current Refresh16 performance or energy. In particular, later Joule values must not be attached to these old implementations by case name alone.

Negative and uncertain findings remain visible. H8-b044 throughput decreased by 10.59% relative to its older cohort; two finite-value checks contributed 1.842 ms/image in a separate short profile, without explaining the entire historical difference. A remaining Full-performance gap of approximately 47 versus 51 FPS was not assigned to one proven cause: EMC, internal tactics, timing caches and earlier process library observations were missing. Five representative raw sets and 27 final split sentinels supported finite output parity, not per-frame equality or a new dataset-quality evaluation.

The [original report as plain text](SOURCE_REPORT.txt) preserves the detailed results and printed precision. The recovered [original stage-profile CSV and explanatory reports](stage-profiles/README.md) now supply its stage-profile reference: 161 rows from five short instrumented diagnoses, distinct from final FPS. Their archive bytes and correspondence to the five accepted profile JSON records were checked; raw timelines and frame-level records remain in the external review archive. The [verification addendum](raw_verification_addendum.json) records the original 7 October check of 6,281 payload hashes, sources, 27 split repeats, 18 Full repeats and three controls; it is not a new raw-data verification performed by this publication update.

From the repository root:

```bash
python3 -I -B results/supplementary-studies/yolov7-completion-20261007/extract_reported_results.py --output /tmp/yolov7-completion-reproduced
```

Choose a fresh or empty output directory outside the repository. The standard-library extractor reads only the supplied report, checks 15 medians and 27 ratios at the printed precision, and writes the CSV, readable Markdown table and [provenance](reported_results.provenance.json). Importing the module performs no analysis or writes. The report bytes and all numerical fields remain unchanged. It does not reconstruct stage timings, rerun models or validate unavailable raw arrays. The recovered stage-profile files are preserved original exports and are not generated by this command. The separately recovered [H10 host-format measurement scripts](../../../scripts/hardware/hailo10/README.md) belong to the September format microbenchmarks and do not supply these October stage profiles.
