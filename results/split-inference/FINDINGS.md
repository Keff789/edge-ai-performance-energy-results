# Findings from the current deployment selection

This report is generated from the same selected rows as Tables 2/3 and all figures. 451 identities provide 451 FPS and 450 energy observations, including all 42 Full deployments. Numeric dataset quality covers 451 identities. No measurement or quality evaluation was started by this analysis.

Native exceeds Generic throughput in 186/204 paired points and uses less raw energy in 195/203; 185/203 improve both. The point-pooled median Native/Generic ratios are 1.5951 for throughput and 0.6564 for energy. Separate equal-model results in `tables/equal_model_summary.json` avoid weighting models by their number of cuts.

Recommended Table 3 compares each model's global fastest observed Full with its global fastest observed split, selected across both runners and all setups. 7/7 selected splits exceed the global Full's throughput; 3/7 are both faster and lower in energy per image. The companion baseline table uses those exact same split UIDs with the same-host Full as a second denominator; 5/7 rows use different Full UIDs for the two baselines. No selected split is below its observed global Full reference. Counts use only the available ratios; missing candidates or metrics remain explicit in the tables. Both new figures use these same unrounded selections and denominators.

Across the separate full set of Native splits, 105/204 are faster than the fastest Full on the same host. Against the separately selected minimum-energy Full, 72/204 use less energy. The largest relative same-host speedup is 4.329x for regnet_x_1_6gf H10/b123; its energy gain against that exact same Full is 4.692. The supplementary `table3_top2_native` retains two Native splits per model selected by this relative same-host gain. It is the historical selection method, distinct from the absolute fastest selection in the new Table 3. These are retrospective results over the measured candidate space.

The fastest and minimum-energy Native split differ in 5/21 groups with more than one candidate. Shared optima and the candidate counts are shown explicitly. A small ratio above one is described as a small observed advantage; the analysis does not infer statistical significance from it.

Quality is a separate result axis. The recorded 26 Native and 17 Generic accuracy-loss flags remain visible. Vendor Full quality, model reference quality and every available split score appear in the quality figures. 53 cached Current losses differ from the numerical selected quality reports; both are retained. The Full and split figures display actual deployments with their quality and timed-work differences, including the YOLO26s fast-oracle versus host-tail distinction described in `METHODS.md`.
